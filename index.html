Mojtaba Jafarian Abyaneh

PhD Candidate, Transportation Engineering

I am a PhD candidate and Graduate Research Assistant at Florida Atlantic University, working in the I-SENSE Lab with Prof. Jinwoo Jang. I hold an M.Sc. in Civil Engineering from the University of New Brunswick and an M.Sc. in Earthquake Engineering from the Iran University of Science and Technology.

My research applies deep learning to intelligent transportation systems — Transformer-based multi-agent trajectory prediction for pedestrians, vehicles, and cyclists from real-world LiDAR data, spatial graph neural networks for mobility flow, and urban digital twins for city-scale wind simulation. My work is funded by the NSF (Center for Smart Streetscapes, EEC-2133516) and the NIH (R01AG068472).
